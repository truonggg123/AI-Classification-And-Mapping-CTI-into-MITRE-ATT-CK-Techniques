2. CO CHE HOAT DONG CHI TIET CUA CYBER EDA (DOMAIN-AWARE CYBER EDA ARCHITECTURE)
================================================================================

Quy trinh gia tang du lieu Cyber EDA bao gom 4 giai doan chinh:

--- Giai doan 1: Xay dung Tri thuc An ninh mang & Tap Bao ve Tu vung (Domain Protection) ---
1. STIX v2.1 Knowledge Base: Tu dong tai va phan tich MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise Matrix 
   de trich xuat danh sach ten Ky thuat, Ma doc (Software/Malware), Nhom tan cong (APT Groups),
   va Tu dong nghia chinh thuc cua MITRE (974 tu dong nghia).
2. TF-IDF Filtering: Tinh toan diem IDF (Inverse Document Frequency) tren toan bo 
   van ban CTI: IDF(w) = log(N / df(w)). Trich xuat cac tu co IDF >= 5.19 (tu chuyen nganh 
   mat do cao, it xuat hien ngoai doi thuong).
3. Protected Terms Set (3,636 tu): Gop tu vung TF-IDF cao + thuc the STIX + token dac biet 
   ([CVE], [IPV4], [URL], [FILE_PATH], [HASH]). Tat ca cac tu thuoc Tap Bao ve nay 
   SE KHONG BAO GIO BI THAY THE, CHEN, HOAN DOI HOAC XOA de bao ve tinh nguyen ven cua 
   ngu canh an ninh mang (Domain Distortion Protection).

--- Giai doan 2: Lay mau Tham lam Da nhan (Multi-Label Aware Greedy Sampling) ---
1. Tinh toan tan suat hien tai cua tung nhan tren tap Train.
2. Xac dinh cac nhan hiem (Minority Classes) co so mau < target_count (giai tri MEAN).
3. Uu tien chon cac mau chua nhan hiem nhat va sinh mau gia tang theo thu tu tham lam 
   cho toi khi tat ca cac nhan hiem deu dat du moc target_count.

--- Giai doan 3: 4 Phep Bien doi Cyber EDA (Atomic Data Augmentation Operators) ---
Voi moi mau duoc chon, thuc hien ngau nhien 1 trong 4 phep bien doi voi ty le alpha = 0.1:
1. Synonym Replacement (SR): Chon n = ceil(alpha * L) tu KHONG thuoc Protected Set va 
   thay thế bang tu dong nghia trong Cyber Synonym Dictionary.
2. Random Insertion (RI): Tim tu dong nghia cua tu hop le trong cau va chen vào vi tri 
   ngau nhien trong cau.
3. Random Swap (RS & Punctuation Protection): Hoan doi vi tri 2 tu ngau nhien trong cau. 
   Dac biet, boc tach va giu nguyen dau cau goc cuoi cau (.,;:!?) tai vi tri goc.
4. Random Deletion (RD): Xoa ngau nhien cac tu voi xac suat p = alpha, ngoai tru cac tu 
   thuoc Protected Set.

--- Giai doan 4: Offline Back-Translation (BT - Mo rong) ---
- Su dung cap mo hinh MarianMT (Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-fr va opus-mt-fr-en) de dich chuyen 
  tiep (English -> French -> English) voi Sampling (temperature=0.7, top_p=0.92, top_k=50).
- An toan thuc the bang Masking/Unmasking truoc va sau khi dich.
